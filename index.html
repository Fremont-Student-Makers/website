About Us

Our Mission

Fremont Student Makers helps high school students do real engineering before college by giving them access to collaborative teams, ambitious projects, mentorship, and a community that takes student builders seriously.

Our Story

One of humanity's earliest desires is to build things. Whether it is a sand castle, a LEGO set, an electronics kit, or the Great Pyramids of Giza, people want to create things they can be proud of.

Many of us grew up with that same drive, but school often asked us to watch, write, or follow instructions instead of building firsthand. We saw students with serious engineering ambitions hit the limits of what school resources could support.

So students from across Fremont came together to create Fremont Student Makers. Today, we connect builders across four area high schools, reach more than 1,000 students, and help new members learn the engineering design process by doing it themselves. Our long-term goal is to create a student-run makerspace that becomes a lasting part of student development in Fremont.
