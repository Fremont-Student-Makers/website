Why this page exists

Make the hub useful, not just visible.

Visitors should quickly understand what they can browse, what is currently live, and what they can do next. This page should make it easy to explore, submit, or start a portfolio.

  • Browse teams and student work without hunting for links.
  • See enough detail to know whether a project is worth clicking.
  • Move straight into the template or request form when ready.

Want your own maker page?

Use the portfolio starter template, then submit your page request so we can publish it under the shared domain. Include real images, a short build summary, and one clear next step.

How to use this hub

1. Browse

Start with the featured project, then scan teams and portfolios to find the kind of work you want to see.

2. Compare

Look for project status, focus area, and proof points so you can tell which pages are current and useful.

3. Act

When you are ready, open the template or submit a request to publish your own page.
